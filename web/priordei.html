Priordei

Where heritage meets High-Conversion UX

UX strategy/ Information architecture/ Interface design

Bringing premium oil to the digital top shelf.

Redesigning the e-commerce experience to turn Priordei's rich heritage into intuitive, high-converting customer journeys.

Product

Translating physical luxury into a high-converting e-commerce. Priordei needed a digital flagship store that reflected its heritage and premium craftsmanship.

Challenge

The existing online store struggled to engage users and suffered from zero conversions. The platform lacked visual hierarchy, transparent pricing, and a clear user flow, resulting in high drop-off rates and missed sales opportunities.

Solution

A seamless blend of brand heritage and CRO. We optimized the information architecture, simplified product presentation, and designed an intuitive checkout flow.

The new homepage:
Blending storytelling, heritage, and conversion

Product catalog & store redesign

From an endless single-product scroll to intuitive filtering, I replaced an exhausting vertical feed with a structured 3-column grid. Introducing category filters, visible pricing, and instant CTAs allows users to scan and shop the full catalog effortlessly

Before

Infinite single-product vertical feed with high scrolling fatigue and hidden pricing.

After

A clean 3-column product grid with category filters, clear sizing, and direct purchase actions.

Beyond the product:
Deepening brand story and origin

01. Home

Architecting a high-converting flagship experience, I reorganized the entire page hierarchy around product discovery and brand heritage to guide users seamlessly from first impression to checkout.

02. Nosaltres

Humanizing a 232-year legacy, I replaced dense text blocks with an editorial layout celebrating Priordei's 10 generations of family tradition to build trust and prestige.

03. Territori

Elevating land, origin, and scale, I paired high-impact landscape photography with key data points (+200 ha) to communicate Priorat's unique terroir and justify luxury positioning.

Reflections & key takeaways

Storytelling drives conversion

Replaced the dense, dark layout with an open, high-contrast structure that gives the product room to breathe.

Structure beats infinite scrolling

Replacing linear feeds with filtered grids drastically reduces cognitive load and speeds up discovery.

Balancing heritage with CRO

Combining a 200-year history with modern conversion patterns requires a scalable, modular design system.
