Maia

Scaling authentic Mexican craftsmanship into a premium brand

Strategic Branding / Luxury Positioning / Pre-Hispanic Symbolism

A 100% Mexican leather goods company specializing in nationwide manufacturing and retail of premium bags and wallets.
Redesigned to scale brand perception, streamline discovery, and elevate digital luxury positioning.

Product

A refined collection of handcrafted bags and wallets built with timeless quality. Each piece brings pre-hispanic architectural geometry into modern fashion, offering a balance of everyday functionality, cultural narrative, and luxury positioning.

Challenge

To create a cohesive brand identity, narrative, and naming strategy deeply rooted in Mexican heritage, positioning traditional craftsmanship as a contemporary, high-conversion luxury brand.

Solution

Geometrized Bespoke Typography: Every typographic element was geometrically structured, drawing direct inspiration from Mesoamerican pre-hispanic architectural monoliths and structural balance.

Role

Brand Designer &
Visual Identity Specialist

Proyecto realizado en sociedad en la Agencia Blank Design Factory.
